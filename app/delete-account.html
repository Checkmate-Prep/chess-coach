Account

Delete your account

You can delete your account yourself, in the app, at any time. It takes a few seconds.

Steps

  1. Open Checkmate Prep and sign in (go to checkmateprep.com).
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Under Your account, open Delete account and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your opponents, names, drill progress and analysis results stored on our server.
  • Your sign-in (email address and user id) at Auth0, together with every other way of signing in that was linked to it (for example Google and a password with the same email).
  • Everything the app saved on the device you used.

It cannot be undone. Other devices keep their local copy until you use Delete account there too, or clear the site's data in the browser.

What is not deleted

  • Usage stats are not tied to a name or email, and they expire by themselves after 3 months.
  • The shared copy of players' public chess.com games holds nothing about you and expires after 180 days.
  • The one-time notification sent to the operator when your account was created, which contains your email address, is held by the notification service ntfy.sh for a short time, and is not part of your account.

Can't sign in?

Write to privacy@checkmateprep.com from the email address of the account and we will delete it.
