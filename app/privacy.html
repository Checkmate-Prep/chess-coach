Privacy

Checkmate Prep works without an account, and most of what it does stays on your device. This page says what is stored, where, and how to delete it. There are no ads, and nothing is sold or shared for tracking.

On your device

Your chess.com username, your opponents, the names you gave them, your drill progress, the games downloaded from chess.com and the analysis of them are stored in your browser or app. They stay there unless you sign in (see below). Delete account in Settings removes them.

Stockfish runs on your device. Your games are not sent anywhere to be analysed.

Games from chess.com

Games come from chess.com's public API, which only has public games. To save chess.com from being asked by every user, the app keeps a shared copy of each player's public games on our server for up to 180 days. That copy has no link to who looked the player up, and no account, address or username of yours is stored with it.

If you sign in

Signing in is optional. It keeps the same opponents, names, drill progress and analysis on all your devices.

  • Sign-in is handled by Auth0. You can sign in with Google, with a code sent to your email, or with an email and a password. It holds your email address, a protected form of your password if you chose one, and, if you use Google, the name and picture Google shares. We never see a password.
  • Sync stores, on our server, your Auth0 user id, your opponents, names, drill progress and the analysis your devices computed (game reviews, trap scans, AI plans).

To remove all of it, see how to delete your account.

AI game plans

When you ask for an AI plan, the app sends your chess.com username, the opponent's, and the statistics built from your games (openings, results, mistakes) to our server, which passes them to Claude, made by Anthropic, to write the plan. A plan is kept for 30 days so identical statistics aren't written twice. The app asks you to agree before the first plan is written, and remembers your answer on that device. To keep the daily limit on plans fair, the server counts plans per IP address; that counter is kept for 2 days. Without AI prep, nothing here is sent.

Usage stats

The app records which screens are opened and when, with a random id for the device, the country the request came from, and your account id when you are signed in. It never records your games, usernames, opponents or IP address. These are kept for 3 months. Turn them off with Share usage stats in Settings.

Who else handles data

  • Cloudflare runs the website and stores the data above. Like any host, it sees the IP address of each request. It also receives mail sent to the address below.
  • GitHub hosts a second, static copy of the app (checkmate-prep.github.io) with no accounts. Like any host, it sees the IP address of each request.
  • Auth0 (Okta) handles sign-in, and an email service it works with sends the sign-in codes, so it sees your email address and the code.
  • Google handles "Sign in with Google", if you choose it. Google Fonts and Google Play are covered below.
  • ntfy.sh delivers a one-time notification to the operator's phone when an account is created. It contains your email address and how you signed in.
  • Anthropic writes AI plans, as above.
  • Google Fonts sends the fonts the pages use, so Google sees the IP address of the request. If you install the Android app from Google Play, Google's own terms apply to the install.
  • chess.com is asked for public games from your device or from our server.
